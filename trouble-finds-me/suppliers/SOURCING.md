## 2. Printing options

| Path | Best for | Cost shape (estimate) | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Local screen printer** | Production run (25–250) | $20–50 per screen setup + per-piece price falling with quantity ([ooShirts guide](https://www.ooshirts.com/blog/how-much-does-t-shirt-printing-cost/), [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/custom-t-shirts-cost-in-bulk)) | Best durability and vintage look, can see a press proof, pickup saves freight | Needs real separations. Minimums are often 24+ per design. |
| **Online screen printer** (Rush Order Tees, Custom Ink, ooShirts) | Same, if no good local shop | Instant online quotes. Example: Rush Order Tees cites a 3-color front on a premium black tee falling from about $13–14 at 25 to under $9 at 300 ([ROT](https://rushordertees.com/blog/how-much-custom-t-shirts-cost)) | Price transparency, stock heavyweight blanks | Less control over distressed textures. Shipping adds time. |
| **DTF (direct-to-film)** | Samples and prototypes (1–10) | Estimate $6–12 per 12"×14" transfer plus press | No setup, full color, can print the AI art almost as-is | Feels like a plastic layer on heavy cotton, cracks over time. Not right for the premium product. |

**Recommendation:**
1. **Now:** order 1–2 **DTF samples** on the chosen Shaka blank to check fit and art scale, and use them for real product photos (about $30–60).
2. **Production:** a **local screen printer** (or Rush Order Tees as backup) running **front 1 color + back 3 colors with underbase**, printing only preorders plus about 10%.

## 3. Artwork blocker (affects every printer)
The supplied art is an AI raster mockup at about 40 DPI over the back print (see `../brand/ARTWORK_AUDIT.md`). Screen printers need vector or 300 DPI separations. Budget about $150 for an illustrator to redraw and separate it. That also gives you copyright in the art.
