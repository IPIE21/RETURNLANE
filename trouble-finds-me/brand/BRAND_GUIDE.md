# TROUBLE FINDS ME: Brand Guide

**Positioning:** Old-school bodybuilding culture meets modern oversized streetwear.
**Audience:** Lifters, bodybuilders and streetwear buyers, 18–30.
**One-line pitch:** Heavyweight pump covers with vintage iron-gym graphics and lines that mean something.

## Personality
Rebellious, confident, gritty, a little funny. Old American gym culture, not tech-bro motivation. We'd rather say one true line than ten slogans.

**Voice:** short sentences, plain words, gym slang where it's natural. Confident, never corny. No "unleash your inner beast," no "grind" clichés, no fake scarcity.

## Type (website)
- **Display:** Anton (condensed block, all caps)
- **Script accent:** Yellowtail (for words like *Tiger*, use sparingly)
- **Body:** Archivo
- **Labels:** JetBrains Mono, uppercase, letter-spaced

On shirts, use collegiate block + baseball script, drawn by the illustrator, not typed fonts.

## Logo
Not designed yet. Interim wordmark: **TROUBLE FINDS ME** in Anton. Recommended next asset: a small chest/neck-tag mark (e.g., a tiger head in a circle, or a "TFM" monogram).

## Product rules
- Big graphic on the back, quiet front.
- Heavyweight, boxy, dropped shoulder.
- Never show AI images as product photos. Label them "concept artwork" until real samples are photographed.
