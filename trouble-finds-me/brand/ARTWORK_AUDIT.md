## Findings
1. **Not print-ready.** The back graphic is about 500 px wide inside the mockup, roughly **40 DPI at 12"**. Screen printing needs vector or at least 300 DPI at print size, separated by ink color. *Status: blocker for production, fine for website concepts.*
2. **It's a mockup, not artwork.** The design sits on a shirt photo with folds and shading. Clean, flat artwork on a transparent background has to be produced.
3. **Color count.** The orange version reads as cream + burnt orange + black (shirt), with some tan midtones in the muscles. A printer will likely need 3 inks + underbase, or a simulated-process job. The planned gold for lettering isn't in this file yet.
4. **No red/cream version of the current text exists.** The red file is the older *Bad Boy / Lifting Club* layout. To compare red/cream against orange/gold/cream for Drop 001, both have to be generated from the same final art.
5. **Open edit you asked for:** turn the shoulder dagger into a flat tattoo. Not done yet, since we don't edit the mascot without approval.
6. **Rights.** Purely AI-generated images generally can't be registered for US copyright. An illustrator redraw creates protectable artwork. Run a reverse image search (Google Lens) on the rider and tiger before printing.
7. **Content note.** The rider has a cigarette. That's fine for organic posts, but Meta and TikTok ad review may limit tobacco imagery. Keep a toothpick version ready for paid ads.

## Colorway evaluation
| | Red / cream (primary brand palette) | Burnt orange / gold / cream |
|---|---|---|
| Fit with art | Classic varsity, but close to the Bulldog-club look that inspired it | Orange matches the tiger, and the gold lettering separates text from the mascot |
| Differentiation | Low: red/cream is common in collegiate gym merch | High: few gym brands use orange/gold |
| Print cost | 2 inks + underbase | 3 inks + underbase (about +$1/shirt at 25) |
| Recommendation | Use for Drop 002 or a club collab | **Drop 001 colorway** |

## Exactly what to ask the illustrator for
- Redraw the back from the orange mockup **without changing** the mascot pose, layout or text. Only change: dagger becomes a flat tattoo.
- Vector (AI/EPS/SVG) + 300 DPI transparent PNG at 12"×14" (back) and 10" wide (front).
- Separated by ink: cream, burnt orange #D4621B, old gold #C9A227, plus a white underbase layer.
- A second colorway file: red #C8102E in place of orange and gold.
- Written transfer of copyright to you.
