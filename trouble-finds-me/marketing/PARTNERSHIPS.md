# Influencers, Ambassadors & Referrals

> Sending any of these needs approval. Nothing has been sent.

## Influencer outreach templates

**Target:** gym creators with 3k–50k followers, real training content, engaged comments. Track them in a sheet: handle, followers, avg views, contacted date, reply, outcome.

**DM 1: free shirt, no strings**
> Yo [name], been watching your [specific video] — the [detail] was crazy. I'm launching a small heavyweight gym brand (old-school iron graphics). Can I send you our first tee? No posting required. If you like it, cool. What size are you?

**DM 2: collab / commission**
> Hey [name], I run Trouble Finds Me, heavyweight pump covers with vintage gym art. I'd love to send you Drop 001 and give your followers a code. You'd get [15]% of every sale on your code. Interested? Happy to send the shirt first.

**Follow-up (5 days later, once only)**
> Hey [name], just bumping this. Offer stands whenever. 🐅

**Email version (for creators with a business email)**
> Subject: Free heavyweight tee + affiliate code for [name]
> Hi [name], I'm [your name], founder of Trouble Finds Me, a new gym-streetwear brand (old-school bodybuilding graphics on heavyweight boxy tees). I'd like to send you our first release and set you up with a code that pays [15]% commission. No posting required for the shirt. Just let me know your size and shipping address if you're in.

**Disclosure rule:** any creator who posts after receiving free product or commission must tag it as #ad, #gifted or a paid-partnership label (FTC).

## Gym ambassador program: "Tiger Crew"
- **Who:** 5–10 regular lifters at local gyms and college lifting clubs. Real gym-goers, not just big accounts.
- **They get:** a free shirt each drop, a personal code (10% off for buyers), **$5 per sale** or store credit, and first look at new designs.
- **They do:** wear it at the gym, post 2 stories a month tagging the brand, and bring in feedback on fits and quotes.
- **Club collabs:** for college lifting and bodybuilding clubs, offer a custom club run (their name + our art style) and a club discount on Drop 001.
- **Admin:** Shopify discount codes per ambassador and a monthly payout sheet.

**Ambassador invite**
> Hey [name], you're one of the most consistent people at [gym]. I'm starting Trouble Finds Me and want a small crew repping it. You'd get every drop free, a code for your friends, and $5 for every shirt sold on it. Want in?

## Referral incentive proposal
- **Mechanic:** every customer gets a personal link after purchase. A friend buys through it → the friend gets **$5 off**, the referrer gets **$5 store credit**. 3 referrals = free shirt from the next drop.
- **Tooling:** Shopify app (e.g., a referral app with a free tier) or manual codes for Drop 001.
- **Waitlist version (pre-launch):** "Move up the list" isn't needed for 25 units. Use simple "share the link" stories instead.
- **Cost check:** at a 25-unit run, contribution is about $13.86/shirt. A $10 total referral cost leaves about $3.86, which is fine for acquisition and not sustainable at scale. Revisit at 50+ units.
