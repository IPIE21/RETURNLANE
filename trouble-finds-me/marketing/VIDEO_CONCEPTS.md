# 15 Short-Form Video Concepts (IG Reels / TikTok, 7–30s, vertical)

Shoot on a phone, natural gym light, real people. Hooks are on screen in the first second. Concept art gets labeled on screen ("concept art, samples coming").

| # | Title | Hook (on-screen text) | Shots | Audio | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Notebook to brand | "I'm starting a clothing brand from a notebook of gym quotes" | Notebook pages → phone with concept art → you at the rack | Talking + lo-fi beat | "Waitlist in bio" |
| V2 | Chew glass, breathe smoke | "The worst set of your life needs a shirt" | Grinder set, slow-mo grimace → back graphic | Heavy trending audio | "Ride the tiger" |
| V3 | Pump cover reveal (concept) | "Pump cover on… pump cover off" | Hoodie or plain tee → cut → concept art on screen | Beat drop at reveal | "Real one coming" |
| V4 | Why heavyweight | "Thin gym shirts are a crime" | Hold a thin tee up to light vs heavy blank (once you have one) | Voiceover | "Waitlist" |
| V5 | Old gyms had better shirts | "Old gym shirts went harder. Here's why." | Vintage gym photos (licensed or your own) → your design | Voiceover | Follow |
| V6 | Small brand math | "What it costs to make 25 shirts" | Whiteboard of real numbers (once quotes exist) | Voiceover | "Preorders = I don't guess" |
| V7 | Tomorrow. | Black screen: "Tomorrow. 7pm." | 3 flash cuts of the art | Single hit | Notifications on |
| V8 | Launch | "Drop 001 is live" | You walking into the gym, turn, back graphic | Hype audio | "Link in bio" |
