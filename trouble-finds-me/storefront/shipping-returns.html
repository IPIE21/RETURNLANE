Policies

Shipping & returns

Draft terms for Drop 001. Final terms are confirmed before preorders open.

Preorders

Drop 001 is made to order. Preorders are open for a limited window. When it closes, we send the run to print and email you an update.

Shipping window: If printing is delayed, we'll email you with the new date and you can cancel for a full refund.

Shipping

US only for Drop 001. Shipping cost is shown at checkout. You'll get a tracking number by email when your order ships.

Cancellations

Cancel any time before printing starts for a full refund to your original payment method. Contact us with your order number.

Returns & exchanges

Because each shirt is printed to order, we can't take returns for change of mind once printing starts. If your shirt arrives damaged, misprinted, or in the wrong size, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll replace it or refund you.

Size exchanges are offered while spare stock lasts. Unworn, unwashed, within 14 days.

Payments

Payments are processed by our checkout provider. We never see or store your card details.
