What is a preorder?

You reserve your shirt during a short preorder window. When the window closes we print the whole run, then ship. It lets a small brand make exactly what people want instead of guessing.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before printing starts for a full refund. Once printing starts, the order is final because each shirt is made for you, though defects are always covered.

How does it fit?

Oversized and boxy with dropped shoulders. See the size guide.

How heavy is the shirt?

Heavyweight cotton. We're confirming the exact blank and weight with samples and will list it on the product page before preorders open.

Is the artwork exactly what I'll get?

The images are concept artwork. The printed shirt follows the same design. We'll post real photos of samples before preorders open.

Do you ship internationally?

Drop 001 ships within the US. If you're outside the US, join the waitlist and tell us where; it helps us plan.
